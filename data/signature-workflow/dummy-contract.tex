This Consulting Services Agreement (the ``Agreement'') is made on 30 March
2026 between Northstar Example GmbH (the ``Client'') and Alex Morgan (the

\section*{1. Services}
The Consultant will provide document-processing and workflow-design services
requested by the Client. The parties will agree on each task and its expected
deliverables in writing before work begins.

\section*{2. Fees and expenses}
The Client will pay a demonstration fee of EUR 1,200 within fourteen days after
receiving an invoice. Expenses require the Client's written approval in advance.

\section*{3. Confidentiality}
Each party will use reasonable care to protect non-public information received
from the other party and will use that information only to perform this
Agreement.

\section*{4. Term and termination}
This Agreement starts on 30 March 2026 and ends on 30 April 2026. Either party
may end it by giving seven days' written notice.

\section*{5. Entire agreement}
This demonstration Agreement records the complete understanding of the parties
for this sample engagement. Any change must be written and accepted by both
parties.
